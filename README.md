# Growth Audit — ClaimHunt

**Live:** https://dkobzar-12345.github.io/claimhunt-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic ClaimHunt already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in ClaimHunt's own design. Only the sequence changes; the art direction is untouched.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
|---|------|-------------------|
| 01 | Show their brands before the price | CR +10–20% |
| 02 | Name their brands in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Give the paywall a way out, and one offer behind it | ARPU +10–15% |
| 04 | Ask for tracking and alerts after the paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Put your 4.8 and your reviews on the paywall | CR +10–15% |

**Grounding.** Every price, rating, review and claim comes from ClaimHunt's own US App Store listing and in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for ClaimHunt.

**Run locally:** `python3 -m http.server 8000` in this folder, then open http://localhost:8000/
